Challenge Track Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026

Challenge Track is an iPhone and Apple Watch app, published by Dawid Pachla, that shows your progress toward a monthly activity challenge. In short: the app has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Your data stays on your own devices and the developer never receives it.

Health data

With your permission, Challenge Track reads the following from Apple Health: your activity rings and their goals, active energy, exercise minutes, stand hours, walking and running distance, and workouts. It uses them only to work out your progress toward the challenge you entered. The app reads Health data and never writes to it.

Health data is processed on your device. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising, marketing or data mining, or sold or shared with third parties.

You can change or withdraw this permission at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Challenge Track.

What the app stores

The app stores on your device the challenge you entered, your progress, the amount recorded for each day of the challenge month, your history of past challenges, and your reminder settings. This is kept in the app's own storage, where the app's widgets can read it.

If you use the Apple Watch app, your iPhone sends the challenge and your latest progress directly to your own paired watch.

If you back up your iPhone or Apple Watch, for example with iCloud Backup, the app's stored data may be included in that backup. Backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and the developer has no access to them.

Purchases

Challenge Track offers a free trial and a one-time purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your name, Apple Account, payment details or any information that identifies you; Apple provides only overall sales figures.

Notifications

Reminders are optional and are created on your device. No notification is sent from a server.

Tracking and third parties

The app does not track you, contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or other third-party code, and does not use cookies.

Deleting your data

You can remove the current challenge in the app, which also removes it from your history. Deleting the app from your iPhone and Apple Watch removes everything it has stored. Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing for the developer to delete.

Children

Challenge Track is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
