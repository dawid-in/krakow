Must-Try Local Specialties

🍲 Żurek

Sour rye soup, usually loaded with sausage and hard-boiled egg. Often served inside a hollowed-out bread bowl.

🥟 Pierogi Ruskie

The classic Polish dumpling filled with potato and farmer's cheese, usually topped with caramelized onions.

🥔 Placki Ziemniaczane

Crispy, deep-fried potato pancakes. Amazing on their own with sour cream, or smothered in rich meaty goulash.

🥬 Gołąbki

Hearty cabbage rolls tightly stuffed with a mix of minced meat and rice, baked in a sweet/savory tomato sauce.

🥘 Bigos

Known as "hunter's stew"—a rich, slow-cooked blend of sauerkraut, various meats, and wild mushrooms.

🧀 Oscypek

A smoked sheep's milk cheese native to the Tatra mountains. Best eaten hot off a grill with a dollop of cranberry jam.

🍹 Kompot

A traditional, non-alcoholic homemade fruit drink (often served at milk bars) made by boiling seasonal fruits in water.

🥃 Nalewka

Potent, beautifully colored traditional Polish vodka infused with fruits, spices, or herbs (like cherry, quince, or walnut).
