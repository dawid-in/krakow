Split Future Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Split Future is a demo app for iPhone published by Dawid Pachla. It shows how a money app could help people split their money between a pension, savings and spending. It is not a real financial product: all balances, offers and transactions are sample data, and no real money moves.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app makes no network requests and sends no data to the developer or to anyone else. It contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking.

What stays on your device

The app saves your demo state on your iPhone so it is still there when you reopen it. This includes the first name you enter, your age, your rough income or term budget, your choices, and the sample balances and activity. The sign-up screen is a simulation: the last name, email address, mobile number and date of birth shown there are not stored or sent anywhere, and no text message is sent.

You can erase everything at any time with Settings > Reset demo inside the app, or by deleting the app.

TestFlight

If you install the app through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with the developer. Apple's own privacy policy covers that data.

Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
